The Plan · Four Components

What we're recommending.

A pay structure built around four levers — team alignment, margin protection, top-end motivation, and per-unit upsells.

01
Team Bonus

Everyone wins when the store wins.

  • 1% of monthly revenue, paid to the team pool
  • Only pays if the store hits its revenue target and minimum gross margin floor
  • Split by hours worked or role-weighted participation
02
Gross Profit Kicker

Pay only on the profit you didn't expect.

  • 3% of gross profit above the monthly baseline
  • Protects against paying extra commission on revenue that was already forecasted
  • Aligns the team to margin, not just topline
03
Over-Performance Echelons

The harder you push past target, the bigger the cut.

Tied to personal or store sales targets. Only pays if the margin floor is hit.

04
Premium Upgrade SPIFs

Per-unit cash for the upgrades that matter.

  • $5 – $10 per qualified upgrade
  • Only paid if the job's margin stays above the floor
  • Museum glass
  • Mat upgrades
  • Preservation package
  • Expedited jobs

Why It Works

Four mechanisms, working together.

Inclusion
Non-commission-driven employees still get team upside through the pool — protects culture while raising the bar.
Motivation
Top performers get a real reason to push past target — the curve steepens exactly where it should.
Owner Protection
You only pay extra on over-performance, not on baseline revenue you were already going to book.
Margin Discipline
The margin floor prevents the team from discounting their way into "fake" growth — every payout is gated.
